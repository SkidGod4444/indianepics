Bana's Harshacharita, written some eight centuries later, says Pushyamitra struck down his master while parading the army before him. The Puranas give the Mauryas 137 years, which from a start around 321 BCE puts the end near 185.

Half a century after Ashoka the empire had shrunk to its Gangetic heartland. The north-west fell to the Indo-Greeks and the Deccan to the Satavahanas; that plural map is where the next chapter begins.
