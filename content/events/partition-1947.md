Cyril Radcliffe's awards were ready before 15 August but published only on the 17th. Working from the censuses, Bharadwaj, Khwaja and Mian estimate 14.5 million arrivals against 17.9 million departures, leaving some 3.4 million unaccounted for; estimates of the dead run from about 200,000 to two million.

One of the largest forced migrations in history, and the reason this site's modern boundary layer needs a 1947 slice and a 1971 slice, not one.
