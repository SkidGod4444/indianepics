The Valmiki Ramayana opens in Ayodhya, capital of the Ikshvaku kings of Kosala, and returns there for Rama's coronation.

Excavations by B. B. Lal in the late 1970s and by the ASI in 2003 found occupation going back at least to the Northern Black Polished Ware phase of the first millennium BCE. The Treta Yuga of tradition lies far beyond anything archaeology can date, which is why the two sit on separate tracks here.
