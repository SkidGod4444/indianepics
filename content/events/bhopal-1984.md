About 40 tonnes of methyl isocyanate escaped from a storage tank and drifted over the settlements around the plant and into the old city. The Madhya Pradesh government puts the deaths from the gas at 3,787; independent estimates run far higher, and hundreds of thousands of people were injured.

In 1989 the Supreme Court approved a settlement of 470 million dollars between the Government of India and Union Carbide, criticised then and since as too small. The site has never been fully cleaned up, and contamination of the groundwater around it is still argued over in court.
