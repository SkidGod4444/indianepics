The first new state since Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Uttarakhand in 2000. The Andhra Pradesh assembly voted to reject the bill in January 2014; under Article 3 a state legislature's views are sought but do not bind Parliament, which passed the bill the next month.

Hyderabad's term as the shared capital ended on 2 June 2024. The 2019 reorganisation of Jammu and Kashmir left 28 states.
