economy:
  intro: >-
    Angus Maddison's final estimates put India near a third of the world's output and people in 1 CE, and at 4.2 percent
    of world GDP in 1950. India's GDP still grew in absolute terms, from about $91 billion in 1700 to $204 billion in 1913 in 1990 international dollars, both for the undivided subcontinent, while its share fell. Part of that fall is Western industrialisation, which made the rest
    of the world grow far faster, and part is India's falling share of world population, so the two lines belong together.
    The points from 1950 cover post-Partition India only: with Pakistan and Bangladesh added, the same file gives about 5.1 percent of GDP and 17.6 percent of people for 1950. The early figures are assumptions more than measurements: the file gives India $450 a
    head in 1 and 1000 and $550 in 1500, 1600 and 1700. Broadberry, Custodis and Gupta build GDP per head from output data and
    find India falling from over 60 percent of Britain's level in 1600 to 14.5 percent in 1871. That decline starts under late
    Mughal rule and carries on under the Company, so they say it cannot be attributed to colonialism alone. Over those years
    India's own GDP per head fell by about 23 percent, while Britain's more than tripled.

drain:
  intro: >-
    There was a transfer out of India that Indians did not control: revenue raised in India paid pensions, salaries, debt and
    other Home Charges in London, under a government they did not elect. Its size is genuinely disputed. The authors below differ
    less on the arithmetic than on what counts: whether all of an export surplus was a loss, whether payments for loans and
    services bought anything of value, and whether the sums should be compounded at all. The $45 trillion figure means little
    without its method, so it is shown here with its method and its critics.
  intro_hi: >-
    भारत से धन का ऐसा प्रवाह सचमुच हुआ जिस पर भारतीयों का कोई नियंत्रण नहीं था: भारत में वसूला गया राजस्व लंदन में पेंशन, वेतन, कर्ज़ और
    दूसरे होम चार्जेज़ चुकाता था, और यह सब ऐसी सरकार के हाथ में था जिसे भारतीयों ने नहीं चुना था। इसका आकार कितना था, इस पर सचमुच मतभेद है।
    नीचे दिए लेखकों का झगड़ा हिसाब से ज़्यादा इस बात पर है कि गिना क्या जाए: क्या निर्यात का पूरा अधिशेष नुकसान था, क्या कर्ज़ और सेवाओं के
    बदले दिए गए पैसे से कुछ मिला भी, और क्या इन रकमों पर चक्रवृद्धि ब्याज जोड़ना ठीक है। 45 लाख करोड़ डॉलर का आँकड़ा अपने तरीके के बिना
    कुछ नहीं कहता, इसलिए यहाँ वह अपने तरीके और अपने आलोचकों के साथ दिया गया है।

    title_hi: एक समान क़ानून संहिता
    body: >-

    Glottolog each draw it differently, so their numbers are not a trend. The Census itself warns that returned mother tongues need not
    match actual speech, and it groups them: Bhojpuri, with about 5 crore speakers, is counted under Hindi. The gap between 1,652 mother
    tongues in 1961 and 121 languages in 2011 is mostly a change in how the Census classifies, not languages dying out.
